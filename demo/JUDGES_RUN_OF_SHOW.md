# Southland RecoveryOS — judge demonstration

Open **http://127.0.0.1:8000/#/demo**. Click **Prepare judge demo case**, then **Open Alex’s workspace**. This creates a real saved case in your browser. Keep the same browser profile/cookies. Rechecking setup adds only missing fixtures; it does not reset your completed tasks or contact history. Rehearsal timings are suggestions; confirm the actual event limit.

## Opening — 20 seconds

“A disaster doesn’t end when the floodwater disappears. That’s when residents face scattered evidence, insurance questions and disconnected assistance websites. Southland RecoveryOS helps them take the next step. Let me show you Alex, a fictional South Holland resident recovering from basement flooding.”

## 1. Overview — 25 seconds

Show the prioritized checklist and 80% documentation readiness.

“Alex is staying with family and needs food referrals. The saved case brings safety, essential needs, evidence and insurance preparation together. This score measures five documentation checks. It never means a house is safe or a claim will be approved.”

## 2. Damage & evidence — 40 seconds

Show the clearly watermarked AI-generated basement image, policy excerpt, estimate and resident inventory.

“These are synthetic demonstration materials. Alex’s own observations stay distinct from AI interpretations. With permission, Claude can interpret the selected evidence and retain evidence references, missing details and uncertainty.”

**Live Claude branch, only after real acceptance passes:** check transmission consent; Analyze with Claude; wait for the actual result; Review analysis. Say: “Here is the real response saved from this evidence. We still verify it against the original.”

**Without a key:** say “Live Claude is awaiting configuration in this rehearsal. I’m showing the verified documentation workflow, and I will not simulate an AI response.” Move on.

## 3. Immediate assistance — 25 seconds

Show 211, shelter finder and food directory.

“Alex’s shelter and food needs guide the resource search. We link to official and established nonprofit sources with review dates. A directory reference does not guarantee an available bed or open program.”

## 4. Insurance preparation — 40 seconds

Save a contact note:

- Organization: **Synthetic Insurer** (must match the saved insurer)
- Date: **2026-11-06**
- Note: **Fictional rehearsal: requested full policy and evidence checklist.**

“The insurer contact is the missing documentation check. Saving it updates the score from 80% to 100%. That means the five evidence checks are represented, not that the records are verified or coverage is approved. The full policy and insurer determination still matter.”

If you already rehearsed in this browser, the score can already be 100%. Say “This saved rehearsal already includes the insurer contact” and show the contact history. Do not delete history or claim a transition that did not occur.

## 5. Assistance programs — 25 seconds

“The South Holland prevention program may be relevant, but Village pre-approval and current terms need confirmation. Federal assistance depends on an applicable declaration and agency review. We have not verified a declaration for this fictional incident.”

## 6. Recovery assistant — 40 seconds, live acceptance required

Check consent. Ask: **What should Alex do next, and which documents still need verification? Cite the supplied official sources.**

“The assistant uses the saved case and source records. It helps identify next steps and questions; it cannot submit an application or decide eligibility.”

If no key is configured, show the visible key-needed state and skip inference. Never read a canned answer as a live response.

## 7. Recovery packet — 25 seconds

Download the actual PDF and reload the page to show persistence.

“Alex leaves with an organized packet: incident summary, evidence register, inventory, readiness, contacts, source references and outstanding prerequisites. Alex can resume the case as recovery continues.”

## Closing — 15 seconds

“Our goal is a resident who leaves with organized evidence, clear questions and a useful recovery packet. We’re ready to validate this workflow with community partners.”

## Quick answers

- **Is it production ready?** No. It is a local synthetic prototype; public authentication and operational/security acceptance remain.
- **Is the image real?** No. It is AI-generated, prominently labeled and never suitable for a real claim.
- **Are assistance matches approvals?** No. They identify requirements to confirm with the agency.
- **What is the evidence of functionality?** Automated API, component and desktop/mobile workflow tests; actual uploads/storage/PDF downloads. Live Claude acceptance is reported separately.
- **Why this community?** The product starts with Southland municipalities and local source references; resident impact still needs a community-led usability trial.
